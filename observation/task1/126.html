About ANITS

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology and Sciences (ANITS) was established in the academic year 2001 under the Anil Neerukonda Educational Society. The institute is approved by AICTE and affiliated with Andhra University. ANITS offers B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA, MCA and Ph.D. programmes with excellent infrastructure, experienced faculty and good placement opportunities.
